# House overrides (Eidos)

Rules that **differ from** or **add to** community anti-slop for our genres.

## Technical / IC prose — allow

- "landscape" when meaning competitive market map with data  
- "ecosystem" when naming a real package/tool graph  
- Parallel structure when listing true enumerations (APIs, flags)  

## Always ban for shelf prose

- Decorative rule-of-three in nutshells and takeaways  
- Dual falsifier walls as the only emotional climax  
- Multi-model scoreboards as the opening of a case study essay  
- "Not X. Not Y. Not Z." stacks longer than one Not-line

## Northstar
