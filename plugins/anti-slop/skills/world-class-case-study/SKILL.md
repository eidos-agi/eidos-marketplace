# World-class case study (prose)

A case study has **two products**:

| Product | May be complete and boring? |
|---------|------------------------------|
| **Archive pack** (deedee/OKF) | **Yes** — lab notebook |
| **Shelf prose** (HTML essay) | **No** — one mind, one point |

This skill governs **prose only**. Load **anti-slop** while drafting. Finish with **anti-slop-audit**.

## Before any prose

### Intent lock (required)

Write and keep:

1. **Point** — one sentence (what is true / what changed / what it costs)
2. **Not about** — one sentence (wrong genre: e.g. not AIC capital underwrite if thesis is "cost of knowledge")
3. **Reader** — who should care after two minutes

If intent lock is missing or wrong, **stop**. Do not fill CHAPTERS.

### Genre

| Genre | Face |
|-------|------|
| Thesis / economic insight | Argument + evidence; multi-source in appendix |
| Capital underwriting | deedee WATCH/PASS/COMMIT; still human prose on top |
| Methods | How we work; not a fake IC memo |

Do not put thesis content in pure Underwriting clothing unless capital decision is the real point.

## Prose rules

1. **One knife.** One recommended story and one mid in the body. Other models/sources → pack or appendix.
2. **Lead with the point**, not with five boards.
3. **Metrics need baseline / after / time** when claiming impact. If unknown, say unknown — never invent.
4. **Trade-offs** — at least one real friction or choice with a reason.
5. **Cut.** If nothing was deleted from the archive dump, it is not done.
6. **Anti-slop** always (banned vocab, no decorative threes, no Not-Not-Not walls as the nutshell).
7. **Pack chrome last.** Nutshell / takeaways / brand / TOC are rails under a real essay — not the essay.

## Recommended shape (flexible, not a prison)

Use when it serves the point; skip acts that don't apply:

1. **Hook** — claim a smart outsider can quote  
2. **Stakes** — what used to cost time/money/attention  
3. **What we measured** — method in plain language  
4. **Numbers that survive skepticism** — labeled self-report vs measured  
5. **What would change our mind** — falsifier  
6. **What to do next** — one path, not a shopping list of tracks  

## Ship gates (all must pass)

- [ ] Intent lock present and matches the piece  
- [ ] Prose is not a dual-board spreadsheet with a byline  
- [ ] Multi-source detail lives in pack/appendix unless one source *is* the story  
- [ ] `anti-slop-audit` run; no unfixed high LEX/STR spam  
- [ ] Human or adversarial voice check: "does this sound like a committee of risk robots?"  
- [ ] Chrome checker (`check-casestudies`) only **after** the above  

## Explicitly not enough

- Green `check-casestudies` alone  
- Complete deedee pack alone  
- Multi-model opinion board as the face  
- Soft rude-qa after HTML without fixing the frame
