title: "The unattended machine is not a mandate"
status: "active"
date: "2026-07-25"
---

A seat that comes up on its own must not act on its own. The only self-directed act permitted is restoring seat availability (its own or its report's). Everything else waits for a human. Violating this turns an always-on convenience into an unsupervised agent with root-adjacent reach.
